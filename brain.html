02 — the point

PLAIN FILES. NO LOCK-IN.

The whole thing is Markdown in a folder on my desktop. If the agent framework disappeared tomorrow, I would still have every decision, every rule, and every correction — in a format that opens anywhere.

That is the part I care about. The AI layer is replaceable. The notes are not. When the vendor bill came due, I did not lose a brain, I inherited a folder. Aiming a second brain at a rented model is the whole bet — and the parts of the stack that decide policy — the gates, the never-do lists, the stop orders — live in the notes where an agent can read them but not quietly edit them out of existence.
