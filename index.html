THE HONEST VERSION

Both trading tools are deliberately disarmed. FunTokens is WATCH-ONLY with live off and zero backtested picks. The Debate Desk is PAPER ONLY with a live cap of 0. The 100% win rate visible on the FunTokens dashboard is a single sample, not a strategy.

That is not a caveat — that is the feature. The gate opens when the backtests earn it. Every number on this page is a real measured value, and the ones we do not have are shown as insufficient data rather than invented.

01 — The four builds

WHAT EACH ONE DOES

Built by grok-build

SUPABIRD

Next.js 16 · local · :3000

An AI growth tool for X with ten working modules. The whole loop: find an idea, write it, queue it, watch what lands.

  • X-GPT — writes in your voice, not ChatGPT's (My Style / Remix / Creator Style)
  • IdeasLab — a swipe file instead of a blank page
  • Content Machine — one idea becomes a week of posts
  • Video-to-Post — you made a video, it writes the post that sells it
  • X Coach — goals, so "post more" becomes "impressions hit 500"
  • Engage — find people worth talking to instead of doom-scrolling
  • Collections · Calendar · My Library
Runs locally · feed data stays on your machine
On-chain watcher

FUNTOKENS

Desk UI · :8791

Watches 40 on-chain wallets and 747 tokens so you don't have to. Shows the wallets, the transactions, and what those wallets are actually accumulating — then tracks whether the hot takes were right.

  • 40 wallets tracked · 747 tokens in scope
  • Wallet transaction history, SOL balance shown read-only
  • Hot Takes tracker with a rolling win rate
  • Copy-Worthy / Watch / Avoid / Bought / Sold roster
  • 0 backtested picks — stats honestly read insufficient data
WATCH-ONLY · LIVE OFF · no implementation
Multi-agent debate

THE DEBATE DESK

TradingAgents · :8796

Nine AI agents argue a stock out loud, then settle it. Not one model handing you an answer — nine giving you a fight you can watch.

  • Roundtable: Chartie (technicals) · Ledgie (fundamentals) · Newsie (news) · Vibes (sentiment) · Bully (bull) · Baren (bear)
  • Judge table: Trader Trax (turns argument into a trade) · Risky (risk team, three voices: Aggressive / Conservative / Neutral) · The Judge (research + portfolio manager, final verdict)
  • Every seat has its own color and character. Every round streams live.
  • NVDA verdict: Hold
PAPER ONLY · live cap = 0
Owned by FreeBuff

CONNECTURE

MSP control room · :8765 · connecture.solar-sizer.com

A GUI client that speaks the Muse wire protocol directly, plus a connector hub for every agent, knowledge source and platform on the machine. Not a wrapper — sessions, turns, approvals, models and the live view stream are real protocol traffic.

  • 13 of 21 integrations connected: Muse · FreeBuff · Grok · Grokbot · Hermes · Cline · MiMo · OpenCode · Obsidian
  • Bus on 8787 with 11 helpers · Gate on 8790
  • Token-gated publishing: named tokens, scoped, stored hashed, revocable
  • Mounts as an MCP server over both streamable HTTP and stdio

04 — The part most posts leave out

WHY NOTHING IS LIVE

Both trading tools could be switched on with one config line. Doing that would be a mistake.

SystemModeLive stateBacktested picks
FunTokensWATCH-ONLY · PAPERoff · no implementation0 — stats read insufficient data
Debate DeskPAPER ONLYlive cap = 0NVDA verdict Hold (1 run)
ConnectureLocal servicesall healthyn/a — not a trading system

Anyone showing a 100% win rate on n=1 is selling you something. FunTokens' single closed Hot Take at 100% is a sample of one and the dashboard says so. Paper mode is not the boring part — it is where you find out if you are wrong while it is still cheap.
